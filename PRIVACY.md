# Vacuula Remote privacy policy

Effective October 19, 2026.

Vacuula Remote is an independent, open-source iOS client for a Vacuula server selected by you. This policy describes the current app implementation.

## Connection to your server

The app communicates with the Vacuula server you select to retrieve robot status, saved maps, rooms, schedules, cleaning history and notification activity, and to send controls you request. That information may include robot identifiers, floor layouts and cleaning timestamps. Live operation requires access to your server; the app does not operate a developer-hosted robot-data service.

Local Network permission enables discovery and connection to a server on your network. If you connect to a different server address, the app sends requests to that address. The server’s operator controls how data is stored and handled on that server.

## Information stored on your device

The app remembers your selected server and robot, cleaning options, map orientation and notification read position. Server responses are displayed in the app. Uninstalling the app removes its local application data under normal iOS behavior; it does not delete robot or server records. Server records must be managed on your server.

## Analytics, advertising and feedback

The current app contains no advertising SDKs, third-party analytics SDKs, or developer-operated telemetry endpoint. Apple may collect TestFlight feedback, screenshots and crash or usage information under Apple’s TestFlight terms and settings, and may share beta feedback with the developer.

If you send feedback or open a public GitHub issue, the information you submit is handled by that service and may be visible to others. Avoid including credentials, robot serial numbers, private server addresses or home maps in public reports.

## Contact

For questions about the app, use https://github.com/tomjtobin/vacuula-ios/issues without posting private information. For private questions, contact vacuulaios@gmail.com.

This policy covers Vacuula Remote, not your Vacuula server, robot firmware, Apple’s services or GitHub’s services.
